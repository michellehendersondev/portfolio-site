About Me

I spend my time in the space where clean code, thoughtful design, and clear communication meet. I’m a visual learner who loves exploring how the web works, solving new problems, staying curious, and finding fresh ways to be creative.


​With degrees in Web and Digital Design and English with a graphic/web design specialization, I build websites that don't just look great—they make sense and guide users effortlessly. When I'm not immersed in web design and development, you can find me reading, at a live show, or hiking a nature trail.
